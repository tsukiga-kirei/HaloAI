# HaloAI product brief

## Product statement

HaloAI is a project-centered collaboration workspace where a team and persistent AI members discuss work, divide responsibilities, produce documents, and record decisions together.

The name evokes a halo, but the product promise is concrete: teams and their AI collaborators share one workspace, turn conversation into outcomes, and keep people visibly responsible for goals, permissions, and final decisions.

## Initial audience

Small product, consulting, operations, and research teams that frequently turn fragmented chat, files, and AI conversations into a shared proposal, specification, report, or project plan.

## First complete job

Several people create a project room, invite specialist AI teammates, provide source material, discuss a goal, assign research and writing tasks, review suggestions, and publish one traceable document.

## Product principles

1. Conversation is the interface, not the system of record.
2. AI teammates are named, scoped, budgeted, and auditable.
3. Human ownership and AI generation are always visually distinct.
4. Silence is a feature: only relevant participants should respond.
5. Documents, decisions, tasks, and approvals outlive the chat turn.
6. Permissions are enforced by code before context reaches a model.
7. Mobile is a focused workflow, not a compressed desktop dashboard.

## MVP outcomes

- Create a workspace and project room.
- Add human participants and configured AI teammates.
- Talk directly with `@mentions` or ask a facilitator to route work.
- Turn a message into a task, decision, or document section.
- Co-author and review a shared document.
- Approve sensitive AI proposals before execution.
- Inspect who or what produced every durable change.
